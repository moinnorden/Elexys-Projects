// ABOUT US

Expert-Led Engineering Solutions

"Dear friends, if you want to do a very well working, presentable project — you should give us a chance to serve you!"

Elexys Technologies is a dedicated electronics and engineering project kit service — turning concepts into intelligent solutions for students across India.

We serve students from Electrical, Electronics, Mechatronics, Instrumentation, and Telecommunication engineering — at Diploma, B.Tech, M.Tech, BSc, MSc, and M.E. levels.

Every project is fully assembled, tested, and delivered with professional documentation, YouTube tutorials, and real-time support via WhatsApp, email, and video call.

Our range: IoT, Arduino, ESP32, RFID, GSM, Solar, Motor Control, 8051, Wireless Energy, Embedded Systems, Robotics, Power Electronics, and more.
